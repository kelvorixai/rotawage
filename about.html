About RotaWage

A small library of plain-English money guides for people whose income runs on a cycle instead of a calendar — rotational, offshore, FIFO, and shift contracts, most often in the UAE.

Most personal-finance content is written for a reader with a fixed monthly salary and a steady routine. RotaWage is written for the mechanics that reader's advice usually skips: fund structures, fixed-term products, and budgeting rhythms that actually fit a cyclical income.

Everything here is general education, not personalized advice, and not tied to any bank, fund manager, or platform. Nothing on this site should be read as a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any specific financial product — for decisions specific to your situation, speak with a licensed financial advisor.
