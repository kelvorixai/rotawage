Why this needs a workbook, not just a calculator

Our free gratuity calculator gives you one number for the numbers you type in right now. This workbook goes further: it holds an HR quote up against the legal formula so you can spot a shortfall before you sign anything, and it runs the same service length against different basic-to-gross splits side by side — which is where the real money in a UAE offer usually hides, since gratuity is calculated on basic salary only, never gross.

Educational tool only — not legal advice. Confirm your specific entitlement with MOHRE or a qualified UAE employment lawyer before relying on this for a real negotiation.
